Empowering Beauty & Wellness

We are a full-service salon and wellness spa of independent service providers. Our mission is to empower our guests through natural methods, wellness, education, and beauty — using eco-friendly Aveda products.

We invite you to a place where passion meets purpose. Allow us to complement your most beautiful qualities.

Services

Hair Color & Highlights
+

From root touch-ups to full transformations, our Aveda-certified colorists craft personalized shades that protect vibrancy for up to 12 weeks. Balayage, highlights, color correction, and more.

Cuts & Styling
+

Precision cuts tailored to your hair type, face shape, and lifestyle. Blowouts, special occasion styling, and everyday looks that last.

Treatments & Consultations
+

Deep conditioning, botanical repair treatments, and personalized consultations to identify the right Aveda regimen for your hair's unique needs.

Massage Therapy
+

Therapeutic massage by independent licensed therapists. Swedish, deep tissue, and custom wellness sessions designed to restore and renew.

Nails & Esthetics
+

Nail care, facials, brow services, spray tans, and microblading from our spa specialists. Head-to-toe beauty in one welcoming space.

Aveda Retail Shop
+

Browse our full selection of Aveda hair care, skin care, and body products — plant-powered, sustainably sourced, and crafted with your wellbeing and the planet in mind.
