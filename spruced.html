The Art of Great Hair

Spruced is a full-service Aveda salon home to independent stylists in Glendale, Wisconsin. We believe great hair starts with the right cut, color, extensions, and care — and a stylist who really listens.

Come in and experience the difference of working with passionate, skilled professionals in a beautiful, welcoming space.

Services

Hair Color & Highlights
+

Color retouches to full transformations — our colorists craft custom shades that keep color vibrant for weeks. Balayage, highlights, color correction, and more.

Cuts & Styling
+

Precision cuts tailored to your hair type, face shape, and lifestyle. Special occasion styling and everyday looks built to last.

Treatments & Consultations
+

Restorative treatments — from deep conditioning to bond-building and keratin smoothing — bring back your hair's natural health and shine. Every visit starts with a personalized consultation to map out the right routine for you.

Extensions
+

Whether you're after length, volume, or both, our stylists are experts in seamless Bellami extension application matched to your hair type — for a natural look and feel from every angle.
